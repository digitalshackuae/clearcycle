Safe and Simple IT Disposal for Schools

Protect Pupil and Staff Data

The records kept by a school about pupils, parents and staff are a concern for schools. It is the responsibility of each school to decide what they want to do with the IT equipment. It is possible that old equipment will still contain records of emails and personal files that the school has created. Deleting a file on a computer may not always be enough to ensure the records remain private.

Our IT equipment removal service offers a secure way for schools to remove old IT equipment. We are happy to pick up old IT equipment that is fit for repurposing but also will come to pick up old IT equipment for secure destruction and data wipe for the equipment that has data storage capabilities. When our team picks up the equipment, we will also leave documentation so the school can keep records and track what has been taken.

Our team can come to schools at different levels (primary, secondary, academies, and trusts) for pickup services. We also offer to collect the IT equipment at times that best fit your schedule in order to avoid disrupting school hours and lessons.

Why are 4 safeguarding rules critical for school collections?

Educational sites hold a strict duty of care regarding on-site visitors. To ensure student safety and protect learning environments, Clearcycle IT enforces four safeguarding rules during school IT collections:

  • DBS-Checked Staff: All attending logistics technicians undergo Enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) background checks. This ensures only background-cleared crews enter the school premises.
  • Escorted On-Site Transit: Clearcycle IT crews remain accompanied by a school representative during hardware removal from classrooms, IT labs, and server rooms.
  • Branded Identification: Every driver wears branded uniforms and carries photographic identification cards displaying their full clearance status for visitor log verification.
  • Planned Out-of-Hours Pickups: Collections are scheduled during school holidays or outside class hours to prevent disruption to pupils. Examples of scheduled collections include summer breaks, half-term breaks, and after-school windows.

These protocols protect school business managers from liability, ensuring that e-waste clearance matches the safeguarding standards mandated by the Department for Education (DfE).

Recycle Old Computers and Laptops

Old computers don’t necessarily need to be trash. If a machine works, with the appropriate checks and a complete data wipe, the computer may be ready for another home. Everything else can be recycled in a sustainable manner.

This reduces the quantity of e-waste and promotes a smarter use of valuable resources. Schools are given more options in determining the most appropriate means of updating IT infrastructures.

How Our School and College IT Collection Works

We keep the process clear and easy to follow.

  1. Step 1: Equipment Details: If you have old IT equipment you would like to have us remove, please send an email with the type of equipment, the site address, and the quantity to info@clearcycleit.co.uk.
  2. Step 2: Arrange Your Collection: Once you have contacted us and provided your requirements, we will suggest a convenient time for the collection. Please let us know if there are any special instructions with regard to your building site and access to your premises.
  3. Step 3: Preparation of Equipment: Staff members will be responsible for placing all items to be removed in one location to facilitate the process. Please maintain a list of devices to be collected, and recorded by us for inventory and security purposes at your school or college.
  4. Step 4: Secure Collection and Data Handling: Equipment is picked up in the agreed location. All data-sensitive equipment undergoes our safe, documented, and proven process. Remaining equipment is then reviewed and appropriately prepared for either further use or safe disposal/recycling.
  5. Step 5: Receive Your Records: Certificates of Destruction will be sent following data destruction and collection. Retain all collection and data destruction paperwork with your IT and waste documentation.
